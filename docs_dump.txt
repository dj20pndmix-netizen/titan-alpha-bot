3 · Apps (developer console)

/developer

app_secret
is shown once on create. OAuth authorize/token and
/ws/v1
are
not implemented

4 · Refer & Earn
Real accounts get a referral code. Share
/?ref=CODE
(any page works). Commission accrues on referred players' real-wallet net gaming revenue; claim to balance via
POST /api/v1/partner/claim
.
